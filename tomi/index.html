Tomi Kauppinen is a computer scientist, author, podcast host and keynote speaker in the Helsinki area. Tomi's passion is advancing spatial analytics and immersive experiences, human learning of timely mindsets and skills, and advancing AI alignment to reach an explainable & responsible AI. The research toolbox for this includes not just philosophical inquiry, finetuning and designing novel methods and training of AI models but also information visualisations, arts & storytelling, design thinking, and versatile approaches from cyber-physical learning and metaverse experiences to cognitive systems and advancements in understanding of AI or more broadly "digital" (risks, ethics, practical solutions).

Tomi is Head, Aalto Online Learning aspiring to design, develop, explore and evaluate a variety of cyber-physical and online learning approaches (via AI Agents, spatial intelligence, geospatial technologies, robotics, telepresence, visual analytics, learning analytics, augmented reality, virtual reality, metaverse, automation, podcasts, videos or games) at Aalto University, Finland.

Tomi holds venia legendi (habilitation, 2014) in geoinformatics from University of Muenster (WWU), Germany, and Title of Docent (Associate Professor) (2014) and a Ph.D. (2010) in Media Technology (Intelligent Systems) from Aalto University, Finland, and MSc (2004) in Computer Science from University of Helsinki. Tomi has been an active voice in the AI field, with a range of roles in the Finnish Artificial Intelligence Society as a board member from 2007 to 2009, as a Substitute Professor of Cognitive Systems at University of Bremen (2014), as a futurist discussing AI ethics, an author writing about our future with AI, and as a member of the International Panel on AI in Education (from 2024).
